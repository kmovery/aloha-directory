About

Island Dental provides dental care to Las Vegas patients. Get quality dentistry services at this local practice.

★★★★½ 4.932 reviews on GoogleView on Google

The story

How long in business
More than 25 years.
Ownership
Independently owned and co-founded by Dr. Michael Tomita and Dr. Stan Askew.
What makes it special
There is a fish tank in the waiting room.

Proof and credibility

Staff training
All staff are trained yearly in OSHA and infection control. The three full-time hygienists are board certified.

Health, wellness and medical

Services
Checkups and cleanings, oral cancer screenings, fillings, crowns and bridges, dentures, whitening, veneers, bonding, gum disease care, scaling and root planing, and emergency dental.
Insurance
Accepts most major insurance plans.
Without insurance
A discount membership program is offered to patients without dental insurance.
Providers
Dr. Michael Tomita and Dr. Stan Askew, plus three full-time hygienists.
Ages seen
Patients of all ages.
Emergency care
Prompt care for urgent issues like toothaches and injuries.
