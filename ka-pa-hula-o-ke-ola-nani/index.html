About

Ka Pā Hula O Ke Ola Nani is a hula hālau at 1651 E Sunset Rd in Las Vegas, led by Kumu Hula Kanani Cadaoas. Kumu Kanani founded Nani Ola Hawaiian Dance Company in Las Vegas in 1994, performing, competing and traveling internationally. In 2021 she completed her ʻūniki under Loea Kalani Akana, earned the title Kumu Hula Laeʻula and renamed her hālau Ka Pā Hula O Ke Ola Nani. In July 2025 she received a second Kumu Hula title under Kumu Kuualohanui Kaulia, carrying the hula legacy of Joseph ʻĪlālāʻole. She teaches students to be living examples of Hawaiian values, passing on knowledge, respect and aloha.
