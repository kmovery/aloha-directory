Hālau Kaleihōkū Kūikanani

📍 Las Vegas, NV
🌺 Own Hālau Kaleihōkū Kūikanani? See what a full listing adds →

Hālau Kaleihōkū Kūikanani is a Las Vegas hula hālau led by Kumu Hula Tiffanie Zuttermeister, who was born and raised in Kāneʻohe, Oʻahu, and moved to Las Vegas in 2014. The hālau has performed at the Pacific Islander Festival in Henderson, and Kumu Tiffanie has been a guest performer in Michigan and at the Aloha Everywhere concert series in Downey, California.
