Your confirmation is on its way by email and text. Before our call, take 9 minutes to watch this. It's the big idea behind My Aloha Map and why your listing matters.

What happens on the call (20 minutes)

  1. We look at your listing together and fix anything that's off.
  2. We ask you a lot of questions about your business: what you sell, your prices, how people order or book, where to find you and what customers always ask. Your answers go on your listing.
  3. We get you verified with the Aloha Verified badge.

It's free, and there's nothing to buy.

Have these handy if you've got them

  • Your menu or list of services, with prices
  • A few photos of your food, products or work
  • Your order, booking or catering links
  • Upcoming pop-ups or events
  • The questions customers ask you all the time

Please be somewhere you can talk for the full 20 minutes. Need a different time? Use the link in your confirmation email or text.
