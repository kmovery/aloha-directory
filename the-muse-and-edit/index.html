About

The Muse and Edit is a Las Vegas wedding and event planning and content creation team led by Rochelle Heikkila. It plans and coordinates weddings and captures candid behind-the-scenes content on the day, and travels for weddings in Hawaiʻi, California, Tennessee and beyond.
