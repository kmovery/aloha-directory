Privacy & Terms

My Aloha Map is a free community initiative of My Aloha Town, listing local Hawaiian, Pacific-Islander, and Asian-American-owned businesses.

How listings work

Listings are compiled from publicly available business information and community recommendations. Any business can claim, correct, or request removal at any time — email directory@myalohatown.org and we'll take it down promptly.

Your information

When you claim, add, or message a business you provide contact details and consent to be contacted. We don't sell your data, and you can opt out anytime by replying "remove".

Saved spots

"My Spots" is stored only in your own browser — we don't collect it.
