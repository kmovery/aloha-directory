Guieb Cafe - Las Vegas

📍 Las Vegas, NV
🌺 Own Guieb Cafe - Las Vegas? See what a full listing adds →

Guieb Cafe is a family-run Filipino brunch spot from Oʻahu that opened its Las Vegas location on W Desert Inn Rd in 2025. The Guieb family runs cafes in Kalihi, Wahiawā and ʻAiea, and they're known for dishes like ube mascarpone pancakes. The family received the 2023 Filipino Entrepreneur of the Year award from the Filipino Chamber of Commerce of Hawaii.
