What customers can't find yet

The story

  • How long have you been in business? When did you start in Vegas?
  • How did it start? What's the story behind the name?
  • Where's your family from? Which island, village or province? Is it okay to put that on the listing?
  • Who owns it? Is it family-run, and who should people ask for?

Contact

  • What's the best number for customers? Is it call, text or both?
  • Who answers it, you or staff?
  • What's the best email? Do you answer DMs, and on which platform?
  • Is a website, booking link or online store live?

Hours

  • What are your regular hours?
  • Do they change by season, or around holidays and big events?
  • Are you closed on certain holidays or for family trips home?
  • Do you do appointment-only or after-hours service?

Location and getting there

  • Do you have a storefront, a home base or a commissary? Or are you online or mobile only?
  • Which part of town is it? Any landmarks, or tips for finding the door?
  • How's the parking? Is it free or paid, and is there a lot or only street parking?
  • Can people get there by bus or rideshare?

Payment and pricing

  • What forms of payment do you take: cash, card, Venmo, Zelle, Cash App, Apple Pay, checks?
  • Is there a minimum, or a card fee?
  • What's the price range? Can you give a typical example?
  • Do you have deals for kupuna, military, students, teachers or first responders?

Who it's for

  • Who's your typical customer?
  • What languages do you or your staff speak, like Hawaiian, Samoan, Tongan, Tagalog, Ilocano, Chamorro or others?

Policies

  • Do you have a cancellation, refund or deposit policy?

Proof and credibility

  • Have you won awards, been featured in the press, or been on TV?
  • Are you a member of any associations, chambers or civic clubs?
  • Are your reviews mostly on Google, Yelp or Facebook?
  • Can we use a customer quote or testimonial?

Community

  • Do you support any causes, halau, churches, sports teams or fundraisers?
  • Do you sponsor events, or donate to them?
  • Do you partner with other local businesses? Who should we link you with?
  • Are you hiring? Do you take interns?

Creators, performers and cultural practitioners

  • What do you do: photography, video, music, hula, fire knife, emcee, DJ, lei making, content?
  • What kinds of events do you take: weddings, luau, birthdays, corporate, graduations?
  • What does a typical booking look like? How long, and how many performers?
  • What's the starting price or range?
