About

OCA Las Vegas advocates for Asian American, Native Hawaiian, and Pacific Islander communities across Nevada. This organization promotes civic participation and emerging leadership.

★★★★★ 56 reviews on GoogleView on Google

The story

How long in business
OCA Las Vegas was founded on July 4, 1998 and is one of 50 chapters of the national OCA, which was founded in 1973.
Known for
The Las Vegas Asian Night Market, first held in 2018 with the Asian Community Development Council, and said to be the only one of its kind in the Las Vegas area.

Contact

Media inquiries email
Media inquiries go to communications@ocalasvegas.org.
Contact form
The website has a contact form for questions and sponsorship inquiries.

Who it's for

Who can take part
Open to everyone in Southern Nevada, of all ages and walks of life.

Proof and credibility

Press features
Featured by Fox 5 Vegas, Channel 8 News Now, Vegas Inc and KTNV News.

Community

Sponsors and donations
They accept donations and sponsors, and list sponsors on the site.

Nonprofits and community groups

Mission
To support and empower Asian Americans and Pacific Islanders to find their voice and identity while making positive changes in the community.
Membership types
Individual, student, senior and family memberships are offered.
Programs
Membership mixers, workshops and panels, volunteering and networking, and a Mentorship Program for high school and college students and recent graduates.
Annual events
Asian Night Market, mentorship program, and JACL/OCA Leadership Summit, plus the OCA National Convention.
Night Market proceeds
100% of net proceeds from Asian Night Market ticket sales support free community resources and services, and vendors keep 100% of their profits.
