About

Inner Visions Tattoo by Mike Rosal offers Polynesian tattoo artistry in Las Vegas. This artist and gallery showcase custom tattoo designs.

The story

Years in business
Inner Visions Tattoo has served Las Vegas since 2017.
Mike's background
Mike Rosal was born and raised in Hawaii and trained under Polynesian tattoo artists there before moving to Las Vegas.
Who owns the shop
Joe Riley is the owner and lead tattoo artist at Inner Visions Tattoo.
Story behind the name
Owner Joe Riley believes every creation starts as a vision in the mind, so he named the shop Inner Visions Tattoo.
Known for
Mike specializes in Polynesian tattooing, blending traditional Polynesian motifs with contemporary elements. The shop is known for fully custom work.

Contact

Call or text
Customers can call or text the shop at 702-732-2200.

Location and getting there

Where is the shop
The studio is a few minutes from the Las Vegas Strip.

Proof and credibility

Awards
Inner Visions Tattoo was named best tattoo shop in Las Vegas by Best of Vegas (Las Vegas Review Journal), gold winner in 2023, 2024 and 2025.

Retail, shops and online stores

What the shop sells
The shop has an online store with art and apparel, including logo tees.
