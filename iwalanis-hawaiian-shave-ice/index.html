About

Iwalani's Hawaiian Shave Ice brings shave ice from Molokaʻi, the Friendly Isle, to Las Vegas. It's ʻohana owned and operated since 2012 and posts its weekly stops on Instagram, at spots like Smith's on E Lake Mead, Tractor Supply and Silverado Ranch Park.
