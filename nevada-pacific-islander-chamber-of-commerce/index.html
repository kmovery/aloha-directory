Nevada Pacific Islander Chamber of Commerce

📍 Las Vegas, NV

Nevada Pacific Islander Chamber of Commerce supports PI-owned businesses and promotes Pacific Islander culture in Las Vegas. This networking organization strengthens the entrepreneurial community.

  • 📞 (702) 460-1646
  • 🌐 Visit website
  • 📍 5000 W Oakey Blvd # A7, Las Vegas, NV 89146
  • 🕒 Monday: 10 AM to 5 PM

Good to know

Where is Nevada Pacific Islander Chamber of Commerce located?

Nevada Pacific Islander Chamber of Commerce is located at 5000 W Oakey Blvd # A7, Las Vegas, NV 89146, Las Vegas, NV.

Is Nevada Pacific Islander Chamber of Commerce Asian-American, Native Hawaiian or Pacific-Islander owned?

Yes — Nevada Pacific Islander Chamber of Commerce is a non-profits featured on My Aloha Map, a directory of Hawaiian, Pacific-Islander and Asian-American-owned businesses.

How do I contact Nevada Pacific Islander Chamber of Commerce?

You can call (702) 460-1646 or visit https://www.nevadapicc.org.

What are Nevada Pacific Islander Chamber of Commerce's hours?

Monday: 10 AM to 5 PM; Tuesday: 10 AM to 5 PM; Wednesday: 10 AM to 5 PM; Thursday: 10 AM to 5 PM; Friday: 10 AM to 5 PM; Saturday: 10 AM to 5 PM; Sunday: Closed.
