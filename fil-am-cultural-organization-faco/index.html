Fil-Am Cultural Organization (FACO)

📍 Oceanside, CA

Fil-Am Cultural Organization (FACO) in Oceanside preserves Filipino heritage through dance, Tagalog language classes, and scholarship opportunities. It strengthens cultural identity for Filipino Americans in the area.
