Their story Website

Gabe and his family are born and raised on Maui, where food was always a mix of cultures. Gabe took those flavors (huli huli chicken, bulgogi beef, kalua pig) and put them in a taco shell.

It started with their son MaCallan. They homeschooled him to be true to himself, and one day he told Gabe: "Dad, you always talked about starting a food truck or restaurant." So he did.

It began with Taco Tuesdays and grew into festivals, car shows, schools, weddings and big events. Friends became crew, and crew became family.

"We feed everyone the same way. We're not trying to manufacture a brand. We're just being ourselves."

Where is HapaHaole's this week?

Tuesday 4 to 8 PM at Khoury's Fine Wine & Spirits (9915 S Eastern Ave), Friday at Pacific Dental (11 AM to 2 PM) and Lavante Community (4:30 to 7:30 PM), and Saturday at Discount Firearms & Ammo (11 AM to 2 PM).

Do they have vegan or gluten-free food?

Yes. The Ban Chan Potato taco is vegan, and the Bulgogi Beef taco, HAPA Corn, Elote Tots and Ube Mochi Churro Bites are gluten-free.

Can I hire the truck for a party?

Yes, for weddings, schools, corporate events and private parties, as a truck or buffet. Send a request on their catering page.

Is there seating?

It depends on where they're parked that day.
